PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: June 11, 2026

Our Privacy Philosophy

Satanic Calculator was built with absolute transparency: your calculations belong to you alone. We believe that your mathematical workflows, financial numbers, and custom formulas should remain completely isolated. Consequently, our application is engineered to function entirely inside your device's local sandbox without any external server presence.

1. Data Isolation & Local Storage

Satanic Calculator does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, inputs, or mathematical results to external networks. All numeric processing, standard memory caching, and scientific calculations happen instantly in the device's volatile memory or secure local storage. We have absolutely no technical means to see or record what you compute.

2. Clipboard & System Integration

The application provides a feature allowing you to copy computation results to your clipboard or paste values into the application. This interaction is handled entirely through standard system gestures. The app only accesses clipboard contents when explicitly directed by you through a paste interaction, and that data is never saved or transmitted outside the app interface.

3. Third-Party Trackers & SDKs

We refuse to dilute our system footprint with intrusive third-party SDKs. Satanic Calculator does not include tracking pixels, targeted advertising frameworks, background location telemetry, or third-party behavioral analytics engines. Your operational behavior inside the app is completely hidden from advertisers.

4. Communications Protocol

If you choose to contact our support team via email for troubleshooting or feedback, we will strictly use your message data and return address to resolve your support ticket. Your personal details will never be logged, aggregated into marketing pipelines, sold, or shared with commercial entities.
